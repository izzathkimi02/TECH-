About our company

Technology built around people.

CANE Tech is a proposed Malaysian student technology company focused on smart cane and assistive mobility solutions.

Our concept combines embedded electronics, connected systems, and intuitive design to explore how technology can support safer and more independent daily movement.

Our Mission

Develop practical assistive-technology concepts that prioritize accessibility, safety, and ease of use.

Our Vision

A future where smart mobility technology is more useful, inclusive, and accessible.
